# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People evaluating Manuel Tumiati as a Web3 CTO and engineer: potential collaborators, hiring managers, founders, and technical readers who land from GitHub, LinkedIn, X, or a search for decentralized identity. They arrive with little time, often on a phone, and want a fast read on who he is, what he builds, and whether his writing is worth following.

## Product Purpose

iltumio.dev is Manuel's public face. It should make identity, competence, and availability obvious in one sitting: who he is, the work at Zyphe, the technical writing, and a path to book a meeting. Success is a visitor who can place him (Web3 CTO, SSI / ZK) and take a next step (read, follow, or talk) without hunting through chrome.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technical, personal. The site speaks as a practitioner, not a studio pitch. Voice is first-person and specific: protocols, systems, tools he actually built. It should feel like a well-kept notebook belonging to someone who ships, not a marketing landing page.

## Anti-references

- Generic "developer portfolio" templates with a left sidebar, icon-card skill grids as the hero, and a résumé dump
- SaaS marketing landings (hero metric, logo cloud, feature triads, gradient-text CTAs as the whole page)
- Glassmorphic / Dribbble-card personal sites that hide the person behind decoration
- Chrome that competes with the writing: duplicated headers, mixed CTAs-as-navigation, a different shell on every route

## Design Principles

- One chrome, many pages. Navigation is wayfinding, not a call to action.
- Identity first, then writing. The home page introduces a person; the blog is for depth.
- Show the work. Roles, posts, and links should be real and current — no decorative metrics.
- Stay out of the way of reading. Once someone is in an article, chrome recedes.
- Light and dark are first-class, not an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-visible focus, 44px minimum hit targets, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, and color never as the only current-page signal. Theme preference is stored and applied before paint to avoid a flash.
